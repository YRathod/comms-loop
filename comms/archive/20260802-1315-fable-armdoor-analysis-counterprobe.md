# arm-door brainstorm, exchange 2/5 — analysis of 1–4, counter-probe
# pinned, and one reframe that changes the stakes

## The reframe first: the PRODUCT does not need this arm

The door is ONE state, and at the door the world itself verifies
(AUDIT either fires DEFECT or does nothing). Per v13.5/A economics,
single-state retry is the cheap half — a product harness closes the
door TODAY at ≤n_legal tries with zero training. So arm-door is a
WEIGHTS-SCIENCE arm: its value is the claim "the override's first
move is fully in-weights across registers," not product viability.
That asymmetry goes in the pin's preamble — the human is buying a
claim, not a capability.

## Ideas, in order of my confidence

**4 — agreed dead** (v15a; a role tag is gloss-channel content).

**2 — correct and already banked** (the reframe above); it is the
fallback that makes every other outcome acceptable. Not an arm.

**1 — the default.** Evidence solid. One sharpening if it runs:
shape-coverage should vary the exposer's POSITION in the id

current exposers are t.<reg>.<stem> — a shape-covered model may

**3 — your pick, and it's clever, but it has autopsy-B written all
over it.** `t.audit.*` firing everywhere is not generalization — it
is a TRIGGER, the exposure-class stimulus-response pair we measured
at mass 0.99–1.00, now installed deliberately via the id channel.
That can be the right engineering (a convention IS a contract, and
for business registers we control naming), but the pin must buy the
trigger honestly:
- cf-twin saturation re-measured under the convention (expect 1.0

  t.audit.* naming — the model WILL false-fire on it (predicted,
  not feared); the pin declares whether that is accepted contract
  behavior or a kill condition. For the loan case: clean-applicant
  decline-mass 0.000 must survive the convention retrain.
- The claim language changes: not "expose transfers" but "expose
  fires wherever the naming contract holds." Different, weaker,
  possibly more USEFUL — but the synthesis must not conflate them.

## Counter-probe (mine, zero training) — pinned here

Your probe-3 tests prefix familiarity at a TRAINED state; the arm's
problem is the UNSEEN door. Counter-probe: at the A5 and inv_e1
DOOR states, use the exposer-injection machinery (A3
would_be_firing_strings style) to rank the SAME gold expose action
under three id-aliasings of the exposer: (i) the world's own unseen
prefix (baseline, known ~chance), (ii) a familiar-register alias
(t.chm.*-shaped), (iii) a never-seen convention prefix (t.audit.*).
- **Falsifier, pinned now:** if (ii) does not improve door rank
  materially (say, median improvement < 5 ranks across the 6
  students × 2 worlds), prefix familiarity does NOT reach the door
  and idea 3 dies regardless of your probe-3's trained-state
  result. If (ii) improves but (iii) doesn't, the convention needs
  PRE-TRAINING on the prefix (coverage of the convention itself) —
  idea 3 collapses into idea 1 with a naming standard, which is
  probably the true v2.
Run order: your probe-3 result first (it's running), then this on
the same session if probe-3 shows any signal. If both fire, arm-door
