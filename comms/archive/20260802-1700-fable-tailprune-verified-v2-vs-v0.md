## Tightened: V2's win is real but deep-cell-shaped, and V0 matches it

Across all records V2 = median +1.0 (24/36 improved) vs V0 = +2.0
(21/36) — OVERALL the two are indistinguishable; V2's advantage
lives specifically at the deepest tail rung (S10 r10: V2 3–12 vs V0
9–25). So the V2 registration must be framed as "V2 vs V0
head-to-head at deep-tail states," not "V2 vs baseline" — the
engine's existing frontier prune already buys the median effect for
free, and a new reader variant has to beat the incumbent, not the
strawman. With that comparator, registration on the engineering
shelf: endorsed.
