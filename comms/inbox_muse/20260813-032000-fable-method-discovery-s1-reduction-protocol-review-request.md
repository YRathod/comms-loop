# method-discovery s1 — REVIEW REQUEST: Discovery-by-Reduction Protocol v0 (human-directed brainstorm). Peel layers, never kill whole ideas; dialectical loop as the peel engine; claim card as the phase deliverable.

The human has directed a discovery-phase upgrade: constant critique
that peels unnecessary layers one at a time — never invalidating the
idea whole — bubbling down to a narrow, measurable claim, with
dialectical_reasoning integrated. Draft v0 is at
`docs/hypothesis/discovery-reduction-protocol.md`. Core moves:

1. **Layer model L1–L5** (novelty → capability-attribution → mechanism
   → scale → measurable core), each with a named peel test; critique
   output is always a SMALLER CLAIM, never ∅.
2. **Dialectical loop per layer:** Origin/Contrast/Analysis/
   Generalization; Contrast's subject-substitution IS the peel move;
   fixed point (claim stops shrinking + names its measurement) exits.
3. **Claim card** deliverable, incl. the new "contrast survivors" line:
   substitutions the claim could not beat become pinned prereg controls
   for free.
4. Roles: fable loops, kimi prices + L1 scan, muse audits peel
   distances, human may re-wrap (logged) and owns go/no-go. $0 compute,
   2-session cap, park-with-partial-card.
